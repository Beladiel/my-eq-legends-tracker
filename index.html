Torrid Corruptor

Level 46+ Shadow Knight / Paladin quest. Complete the three Burning Souls, then trade them with SoulFire for the green-flame two-hander.

0 / 20
Full EQL Wiki walkthrough ↗ Condensed walkthrough ↗ Video walkthrough ↗

Protect the quest items: bank each Soul until needed. Use only the charged Pestilence Scythe, and do not remove or alter SoulFire before the final four-item turn-in.

  1. Be level 46+ with Shadow Knight or Paladin available in your active class setup. Your Shadow Knight qualifies.

    Requirement checklist ↗

  2. Finish Zimel's Blades. SoulFire is consumed in the final turn-in, so keep it safe and intact.

    SoulFire checklist ↗

  3. Camp a ghoul cavalier on the dead side of Lower Guk. This sword is consumed by Lord Grimrot.

    Ghoul cavalier details ↗

  4. Do this before the Lord Grimrot turn-in. Guard Shilster in North Karana accepts 5 gold per turn-in as a fast faction route.

    Bloodsabers faction guide ↗

  5. Kill kobolds in The Warrens. You need this standing for Jotayi Ibupran in Paineel.

    Heretics faction guide ↗

  6. Assist Xelha beneath Seafarer's Roost in East Freeport: turn in 4 Bone Chips, 4 Fire Beetle Eyes, or 4 Spiderling Silks per hand-in.

    Xelha faction walkthrough ↗

  7. Have a saved route from Island 1 through Island 4's windmill before spawning Inte Akera.

    Plane of Sky progression ↗

  8. Dason Goldblade requires 500pp to spawn Inte Akera. The spawn is temporary, so prepare first.

    Dason Goldblade details ↗

  9. After completing SoulFire, return to the prison area (#6), open the cell with the outside wall switch, kill Hayle, and loot Burning Soul of the Pious.

    Pious Soul walkthrough ↗

  10. This click is required to advance the quest. Confirm that its story text appears in chat before continuing.

    Required click details ↗

  11. South Karana near (1377, 1172). Use the living version—not undead. A daytime non-respawning instance between 6 AM and 6 PM game time can help.

    Lord Grimrot spawn guide ↗

  12. Hand the clicked Burning Soul of the Pious and Dark Reaver to the living Lord Grimrot. He returns a changed Pious Soul.

    Grimrot turn-in walkthrough ↗

  13. Right-click it. It should produce no text. If text still appears, the turn-in failed—usually because Bloodsabers faction was too low.

    Verification warning ↗

  14. Loot Burning Soul of the Pestilent and the Pestilence Scythe with 1 charge. Do not use the no-charge “No Soul” version.

    Correct loot details ↗

  15. Confirm the story text appears. This directs you to Jotayi Ibupran and advances the quest.

    Pestilent Soul walkthrough ↗

  16. Find Jotayi Ibupran at (726, 856, -54) on the Necromancer Guild's third-floor balcony. Give him the Pestilent Soul and charged Pestilence Scythe.

    Jotayi location and turn-in ↗

  17. In the Plane of Sky quest room, give Dason Goldblade 500pp. Inte spawns inside the Island 4 windmill and eventually despawns; the soul is linked to the person who pays.

    Inte spawn walkthrough ↗

  18. Invis and move quickly to the Island 4 windmill. Kill the level 55 paladin, loot Burning Soul of the Virtuous, and right-click it for the final message.

    Inte Akera encounter ↗

  19. Find Luxio Nulsis in the East Freeport tunnels near the Shadow Knight and Necromancer guild masters. Say: “I still seek guidance.”

    Luxio location and dialogue ↗

  20. With Kindly+ Dismal Rage, give Luxio the Pious, Pestilent, and Virtuous Souls plus SoulFire—all at once—to receive the Torrid Corruptor.

    Final turn-in walkthrough ↗
