Mane Attraction — Mana Preservation II

New focus target: obtain the ALL-class HEAD item and keep it for Mana Preservation II.

0 / 6
EQL Wiki quest guide ↗

Goal: stop when Ping Fuzzlecutter gives you Mane Attraction. Do not hand it back to Sanfyrd if you want to keep the Mana Preservation II head item. The EQL wiki lists the overall Rain Caller quest as Ranger, while Mane Attraction itself is ALL / ALL.

  1. Find Sanfyrd in Ak'Anon and begin the hair-tonic / toupee portion.

  2. Buy the tonics from Ping Fuzzlecutter in West Freeport and combine all 10 in the crate to make the Crate of Tonic.

  3. Return to Sanfyrd Featherhead in Ak'Anon and receive the Tattered Toupee.

  4. Farm a hulking gorilla in Cazic Thule until you have two Clumps of Gorilla Hair.

  5. Kill a golden haired mermaid in Kedge Keep and loot the Lock of Hair.

  6. Give Ping the Tattered Toupee, two Clumps of Gorilla Hair, and the Lock of Hair. Receive Mane Attraction: HEAD, ALL / ALL, Mana Preservation II.

Torrid Corruptor

Level 46+ Shadow Knight / Paladin quest. Complete the three Burning Souls, then trade them with SoulFire for the green-flame two-hander.

0 / 20
Full EQL Wiki walkthrough ↗Condensed walkthrough ↗

Protect the quest items: bank each Soul until needed. Use only the charged Pestilence Scythe, and do not remove or alter SoulFire before the final four-item turn-in.

  1. Be level 46+ with Shadow Knight or Paladin available. Your Shadow Knight qualifies.

  2. Finish Zimel's Blades. SoulFire is consumed in the final turn-in, so keep it safe and intact.

  3. Camp a ghoul cavalier on the dead side of Lower Guk. This sword is consumed by Lord Grimrot.

  4. Do this before the Lord Grimrot turn-in.

  5. Kill kobolds in The Warrens. You need this standing for Jotayi Ibupran in Paineel.

  6. Use appropriate faction work before the final Luxio turn-in.

  7. Have a saved route from Island 1 through Island 4's windmill before spawning Inte Akera.

  8. Dason Goldblade requires 500pp to spawn Inte Akera.

  9. Loot Burning Soul of the Pious.

  10. Confirm its story text appears before continuing.

  11. South Karana near (1377, 1172). Use the living version, not undead.

  12. He returns a changed Pious Soul.

  13. Right-click it. It should produce no text.

  14. Loot Burning Soul of the Pestilent and the charged Pestilence Scythe.

  15. Confirm the story text appears.

  16. Give him the Pestilent Soul and charged Pestilence Scythe.

  17. Give Dason Goldblade 500pp in the Plane of Sky quest room.

  18. Loot Burning Soul of the Virtuous and right-click it.

  19. In East Freeport say: “I still seek guidance.”

  20. With Kindly+ Dismal Rage, give Luxio the Pious, Pestilent, and Virtuous Souls plus SoulFire all at once.
